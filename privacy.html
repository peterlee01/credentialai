Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This policy explains what happens to the information you submit through the live verification demo on this site.

What we collect

  • The first name, last name, state, and profession you enter into the verification form.
  • Your IP address, used briefly and only in server memory to enforce a one-verification limit per visitor.
  • A single cookie that records whether you've already used your one free verification.

How we use it

The information you submit is used solely to run the verification you requested: it is sent to the NPPES NPI Registry and the HHS-OIG List of Excluded Individuals/Entities (both public U.S. government data sources), and to Anthropic's Claude API, which coordinates the check and produces the verdict text you see on screen.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell or share the names you submit with any third party for marketing purposes.
  • We do not build profiles linking multiple visits together beyond the single rate-limiting cookie.
  • We do not retain submitted names in a database — each request is processed and returned; it is not stored afterward.

Cookies

We set one HttpOnly cookie (cv_used) purely to enforce the one-verification-per-session limit. It contains no personal information and cannot be read by third-party scripts.

Questions

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at info@credentialai.co.
