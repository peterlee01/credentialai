Ongoing monitoring

Credentialing doesn't end at hire. Licenses lapse, and sanctions can appear at any time. Monitoring rechecks every physician on your roster on a regular schedule and tells you as soon as something changes, so you're not finding out at re-credentialing.

Monitoring is part of Pro and Enterprise, and can be added to any plan.

  • Scheduled rechecks of license status and expiry
  • Sanctions screening against the federal exclusion list
  • Alerts when a status changes, with the record behind it
  • A current verification report for every physician
