Description of service

Credentialai checks a physician's or clinician's credentials against public primary sources and uses AI to match verified physicians with facilities. Each verification includes:

  • Identity, checked against the NPPES NPI Registry.
  • Sanctions, checked against the HHS-OIG List of Excluded Individuals/Entities (LEIE).
  • License status, checked against state records:
    • Colorado, Washington and Connecticut, for all supported professions, using those states' public license records (Colorado Department of Regulatory Agencies, Washington State Department of Health, Connecticut eLicensing).
    • Physicians in Alabama, Alaska, California, Delaware, Florida, Illinois, Maine, Maryland, Massachusetts, Michigan, Missouri, New Hampshire, New Jersey, New York, Ohio, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Vermont and West Virginia, using License Verify, a third-party service that reads state medical board rosters by license number.
    • For any other state or profession, or when a record can't be read, the license is marked for a person to confirm with the state board. A license status is never assumed.

From those results, Credentialai gives a plain-language GO, NEEDS REVIEW or NO-GO verdict, written with the help of Anthropic's Claude AI, and a downloadable PDF report.

AI matching suggests similar physicians for facilities, drawn from the NPPES NPI Registry and from physicians who chose to be found, and suggests facilities with open roles for physicians, drawn from public job listings (Google Jobs, via SerpApi). Match scores and reasons are generated automatically from the information available.
