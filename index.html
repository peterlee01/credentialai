What it does

Two sides. One verified network.

Facilities verify physicians and get AI-matched candidates. Physicians get verified once and see the facilities hiring for them.

01 · For facilities

Credential physicians and find AI-matched candidates

Check any physician against primary sources for one clear verdict, then get a ranked list of similar, verified physicians nearby, with the reason each one fits and a way to contact them.

  • Identity, sanctions and license checked against primary sources
  • A plain-language GO, NEEDS REVIEW or NO-GO verdict, with a PDF report
  • AI-matched physicians ranked by fit, open-to-offers first
02 · For physicians

AI matching to the facilities hiring for you, with a verified profile

Claim your NPI and Credentialai's AI matches you to facilities with open roles near you, with pay and direct job links, and keeps a verified profile you can send to any recruiter.

  • Jobs matched to your location, setting, pay and contract type
  • Identity, sanctions and license verified, downloadable as a PDF
  • Let facilities find you, marked open to offers
